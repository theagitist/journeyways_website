Journeyways

JOURNEYWAYS is a research project on identity and agency, expressed as a game of self-discovery through collaborative storytelling and meaningful choices.

Key features of JOURNEYWAYS

No Fixed Roles

Discover your character through play, not predetermined archetypes.

Collaborative Storytelling

Co-create narratives that reveal deeper truths about identity.

Continuous Growth

Each session builds upon the last, creating an evolving journey.

Gameplay features

Solo or Group Play

Whether you're exploring alone or with friends, JOURNEYWAYS adapts to your preferred style of play. Solo sessions offer deep introspection, while group play creates rich, collaborative narratives.

Meaningful Choices

Every decision in JOURNEYWAYS carries weight. Your choices don't just affect the game; they reveal aspects of yourself you might not have known existed.
