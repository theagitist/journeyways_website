Journeyways

A game about becoming.

You don't play to win; you play to unfold.
You choose, and you uncover.

Explore selfhood not with labels, but with stories, memory, and meaning. Whether solo or as part of a group, each session reveals a story made only by your presence.

Design philosophy
Principles

Key features of JOURNEYWAYS

Identity

No Fixed Roles

You don't pick from a list of classes, races, or archetypes. You arrive as yourself, or as someone you invent. Realistic, fantastic, simple, or layered are all valid starting points.

Identity is built through play, not selected from a list.

Design philosophy: Identity is uncovered About the project

Voice

Collaborative Storytelling

A session unfolds as dialogue. Players tell their own stories and respond to others'. Cards prompt encounters, tiles place you on a shared map, and group cards invite the table to weave threads together.

There is no single narrator. The story belongs to everyone at the table.

Read further

Design philosophy: A mirror in a cave How to play

Continuity

Continuous Growth

Sessions can stand alone, or continue. Players bring a character into a future session, open a chapter two, or pick up where they left off. There is no formal mechanic for continuity. What carries is the story.

Solo and group play share the same character. The digital version supports the same migration.

Gameplay

Gameplay features

Solo or group play

Whether you're exploring alone or with friends, JOURNEYWAYS adapts to your preferred style of play. Solo sessions offer deep introspection, while group play creates rich, collaborative narratives.

Meaningful choices

Every decision in JOURNEYWAYS carries weight. Your choices don't just affect the game; they reveal aspects of yourself you might not have known existed.
