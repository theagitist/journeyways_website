Journeyways

You don't play to win; you play to unfold.
You choose, and you uncover.

Explore selfhood not with labels, but with stories, memory, and meaning. Whether solo or as part of a group, each session reveals a story made
only by your presence.

Key features of JOURNEYWAYS

No Fixed Roles

There are no character classes, races, or archetypes to pick from before you sit down. You arrive as yourself, or as someone you invent. Realistic, fantastic, simple, layered: any of these is a valid starting point.

Identity is built through play, not selected from a list.

Design philosophy: Identity is uncovered About the project

Collaborative Storytelling

A session tends to develop into a dialogue. Players tell their own stories and respond to others'. Cards prompt encounters, movement, interaction and introspection. Tiles place you on a shared map. Group cards invite the whole table to weave threads together.

There is no single narrator. The story belongs to everyone at the table.

Design philosophy: A mirror in a cave How to play

Continuous Growth

Sessions can stand alone, or they can continue. Players bring a character into a future session, open a chapter two, or simply pick up where they left off. There is no formal mechanic for continuity. What carries is the story.

Solo and group play share the same character; the digital version supports the same migration.

Design philosophy: Expression in any modality About the project

Gameplay features

Solo or Group Play

Whether you're exploring alone or with friends, JOURNEYWAYS adapts to your preferred style of play. Solo sessions offer deep introspection, while group play creates rich, collaborative narratives.

Meaningful Choices

Every decision in JOURNEYWAYS carries weight. Your choices don't just affect the game; they reveal aspects of yourself you might not have known existed.
