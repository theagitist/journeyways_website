Board game · Rulebook

How to play.

Setup, turns, and how a session ends.

JOURNEYWAYS is not about winning or losing. The rules below are a frame to start from. Bend them, break them, write your own. The single fixed rule is that everyone at the table agrees.

Decks

Decks

Cards are shuffled together, but then spread into three or more piles. Players choose which pile to pick from. They know the kind of card they will get, but not exactly which one.

Red | Encounters

Green | Movement

Blue | Reflections

Black | Countdown

Purple | Group actions

Setup

Setup

What you'll need

  • Board tiles (35).
  • Game cards (81).
  • Player tokens (1 per player).
  • Player booklet (1 per player).
  • Some sticky notes.
  • Die for choosing the starting player (1).
  • Pens and pencils.

Initial setup

  1. Shuffle all cards together and place them face down in three piles.
  2. Pick one card and place it apart face up. This will be the Discard pile.
  3. Find the Starter board tile and set it apart. Shuffle all others and place them face down in three piles.
  4. Each player takes one player token.
  5. Use the die to determine the starting player. For example, highest number goes first.
  6. Place the Starter board tile in the center of the table.
  7. Taking turns, each player picks a tile from any pile and places it adjacent to another previously placed tile. If it's a blank tile, put it back in the pile and pick again.
  8. All players place their tokens on the Starter tile.
Turn

Turn

Each turn has three phases: Explore, Pick, and Reflect.

Phase 1

Explore

Two options. Pick a map tile and place it adjacent to another one. If you pick a blank tile, you have to create it before placing it. Or use a sticky note to draw a new map tile (whatever you want) and place it on top of an existing one where no player is located. After placing your tile, you can move one or two spaces in any direction.

Phase 2

Pick

Pick a card from any pile, even the Discard pile. Green cards are movement, Black cards are pass of time, Blue cards are quotes, Red cards are encounters, Purple cards are group events.

Phase 3

Reflect

If you picked a Green or Purple card, you have to perform the action it indicates. If you picked a Blue or Red card, take into account what they say, and write a new entry in your journal. If you picked a Black card, set it apart. The game ends when the fifth Black card is picked.

Ending

Ending

The game ends when the fifth Black card is picked. Each player can add a final entry to their journal, or just leave it as it is. If all players agree, you can end after any number of Black cards, or disregard them and use a timer, or stop whenever a story feels complete.

It is okay if your story ends abruptly. You can leave it like that, or return to it on another session.

After ending the game, each player can fill in their character sheet at the back of the player booklet. This way they can see how their character developed during the game: what changed, and what stayed the same.

“The game is not about winning or losing. It is about discovering, exploring, and becoming.”

Journal

Journal

Unlike traditional games, your character in JOURNEYWAYS emerges through play rather than being predetermined. Use the cards as inspiration, not rigid definitions; the main objective is that you set your imagination free and write the story you want to tell.

Journal entries can be as short or long, as terse or detailed as you want. You can take inspiration from the tile where your token is located and the text on the card, but you can be as creative as you want. Write a journal entry. Doodle it. Draw a comic. Write a fragment. Leave it blank. All of these count.

Modes

Modes of play

Two ways to play. Choose either, or move between them.

Solo play

  • Remove the Purple cards from the game.
  • Focus on deep personal reflection.
  • Take as much time as you need.
  • Use journaling extensively.
  • Create your own pacing.
  • Return to previous sessions.

Group play

  • Share reflections with others.
  • Learn from different perspectives.
  • Build collective narratives.
  • Support each other's growth.
  • Create shared memories.
Open play

Open play

Returning to a session

JOURNEYWAYS is designed for ongoing play. Return to previous sessions, revisit old insights, see how your understanding has evolved. Your character and story continue to develop between sessions.

Make your own

Add your own story cards, tiles, character elements, mechanics. Templates are open. JOURNEYWAYS is a framework for exploration; make it your own.

Setting the table

  • Set aside dedicated time.
  • Minimize distractions.
  • Create a comfortable environment.
  • Honor everyone's privacy.
  • Practice active listening.

Going deeper

  • Ask open-ended questions.
  • Embrace uncertainty.
  • Allow for silence and reflection.
  • Be curious, not judgmental.
  • Celebrate small discoveries.
